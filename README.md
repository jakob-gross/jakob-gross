# Jakob Groß

Full-stack developer, filmmaker and visual anthropologist working at the intersection of narrative craft and technical systems.

I build AI-powered storytelling tools, automated creative workflows and full-stack applications for narrative, image and media production. My focus is AI as creative infrastructure for writers, illustrators, filmmakers, educators and story teams.

Most of my current product repositories are private because they contain unreleased product code, business logic, infrastructure setup and creative IP. This profile gives context on my work, stack and direction.

## Current Focus

Currently building **Wundercomic**, an AI web app that turns public-domain literature into interactive comics for children.

The project combines:

- automated literary analysis and narrative compression
- multi-phase agent workflows for story adaptation
- prompt orchestration for image generation
- character, scene and style consistency across comic pages
- full-stack product development with secure infrastructure
- storytelling, pedagogy and visual design

## What I Work On

- AI storytelling systems and dramaturgy-aware workflows
- Automated storyboarding, adaptation and narrative distillation
- Image and video AI for creative production pipelines

- Full-stack web applications with React, Next.js, Node.js and APIs

## Background

Before moving deeper into software development, I worked for more than a decade as a documentary filmmaker, director of photography and producer for broadcasters including ARTE, ARD, ZDF, BR, SWR and RBB.

I also worked as a lecturer in journalism, video journalism and filmmaking, and as an associated member of the Cluster of Excellence “Asia and Europe in a Global Context” at Heidelberg University, focusing on visual anthropology and digital forms of knowledge transfer.

That background shapes how I build: I care about narrative structure, visual clarity, users, context and the translation of complex ideas into usable systems.
